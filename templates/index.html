How Blood Donation Works

Volunteering is safe, hygienic, and structured through a strict medical protocol managed by licensed phlebotomists.

1
Registration

Fill out basic medical history, donor profile, age, and blood group verification.

2
Health Screening

A medical officer checks vital signs, pulse, blood pressure, and hemoglobin count.

3
Donation

Relax in comfortable recliners while 350-450 mL of whole blood is safely collected in 10-12 mins.

4
Refresh & Save

Enjoy complimentary refreshments, receive your LifeSaver Donor Card, and track your impact!

Clinical Excellence

Why Healthcare Providers Choose LifeSaver

Equipped with temperature-validated cold-chain transit, barcode sample traceability, and nucleic acid testing (NAT) for pathogens, LifeSaver sets the benchmark for blood safety.

Stringent Safety & Testing

Every donation undergoes rigorous infectious disease screening and automated serological typing.

Cold-Chain Logistics

Temperature-monitored refrigerated dispatch units deliver critical blood to hospitals in under 45 minutes.

Atomic Inventory Auditing

Transactional MySQL database ensures real-time stock integrity and prevents allocation overlaps.
